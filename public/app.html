                Wi-Fi Troubleshooting Tips
                
                  • Ensure PC and Phone are on the same Wi-Fi router frequency (2.4GHz/5GHz).

                  • Disable "AP Isolation" / "Client Isolation" in router settings if connection fails.

                  • If IP changes frequently, assign a static DHCP lease to your phone in your router settings.
