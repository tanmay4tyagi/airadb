Engineered for Developers

Everything you need for cordless Android development

AirADB Studio runs natively on your computer, eliminating ADB friction so you can focus on building apps.

Wireless ADB Pairing

Pair with modern Android 11+ phones instantly using 6-digit codes. Once paired, reconnect automatically whenever your phone connects to the same Wi-Fi.

1-Click USB to Wireless Switch

For Android 9 and 10 devices: plug in via USB just once, click the switch button to activate TCP/IP mode on port 5555, then unplug and stay connected wirelessly.

Remote Controls & Keystrokes

Trigger Home, Back, App Switcher, Power, volume controls, and send custom text strings directly from your laptop keyboard into active input fields.

Live Screen Inspection

Capture full-resolution device screenshots wirelessly in lossless PNG format. Copy straight to clipboard or save for bug reports and PR walkthroughs.

Battery & Diagnostics

Monitor real-time battery levels, thermals, charging source (AC/USB/Wireless), voltage, and power state without installing any companion APK on your phone.

Auto-Discovery & Sideloading

Discover active ADB services across your Wi-Fi subnet via mDNS and TCP/IP probes. Drag-and-drop APK files onto the studio window for instant sideloading.

Quick Start

How It Works in 3 Easy Steps

No complicated SDK configs. Get up and running in less than 60 seconds.

1

Download & Launch

Download AirADB Studio for Windows and launch it. The built-in ADB manager automatically detects or provisions the official Android platform tools.

2

Enable Wireless Debugging

On your Android device, go to Settings > Developer Options > Wireless Debugging and toggle it ON. Tap "Pair device with pairing code".

3

Connect & Debug Wirelessly

Enter the pairing port and 6-digit code into AirADB Studio. Your phone instantly links and opens the full Device Studio dashboard.
